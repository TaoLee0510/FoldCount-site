Requirements

Counting needs iPhone Duo with iOS 27.1 or later. On other iPhones, FoldCount shows the counts synced through iCloud from your iPhone Duo.

Set up automatic counting

FoldCount counts while it is open. To count every unfold and fold, even when FoldCount is closed and after a restart, create two Shortcuts automations once:

  1. Open Shortcuts and tap +.
  2. Search for Automation. Under Display, tap Opened or Closed, then choose Opened.
  3. Search for FoldCount, add Record Fold or Unfold and leave it set to Unfold.
  4. If you see options to ask before running or to notify when run, turn them off.
  5. Make a second automation the same way, with Closed and the action set to Fold.
  6. Fold and unfold your iPhone, then open FoldCount: Automatic Counting should say it last ran just now.

Frequently asked questions

Why did my counts go back to zero?

Without FoldCount Pro, counts live only while the app is running. They are erased when FoldCount closes, including when iOS closes it in the background, and when your iPhone restarts. FoldCount Pro saves every count.

How do I restore FoldCount Pro?

Open FoldCount, tap Unlock Pro and then Restore Purchases, using the Apple Account you bought it with. FoldCount Pro supports Family Sharing: members of your family group get it too.

My counts aren’t syncing

Syncing needs FoldCount Pro, an iCloud account, and iCloud turned on for FoldCount in the Settings app. FoldCount shows “iCloud Is Off” when it can’t use iCloud. Syncing can take a few minutes, especially after a reinstall.

Automatic Counting says it hasn’t run

Open Shortcuts, tap Automation and check that both automations are on. After deleting and reinstalling FoldCount, you may need to add the FoldCount action to the automations again.

The widgets show a lock

Widgets show counts that have been saved, so they need FoldCount Pro.

What counts as an unfold?

Each time your iPhone opens from closed. In Settings › Count you can count unfolds only, or unfolds and folds together.

How do I delete my data?

In FoldCount, open Settings and tap Reset All Counts. With FoldCount Pro this also deletes your counts from iCloud and your other devices. See the Privacy Policy.
