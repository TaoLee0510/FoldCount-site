Privacy Policy

FoldCount for iPhone

Support Privacy Policy 简体中文

In short: FoldCount does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code. Your counts stay on your devices and in your own iCloud.

Effective date: 13 November 2026

Who we are

FoldCount is developed by Todd Leigh (“we”). If you have a question about this policy, write to taoli.toddli@gmail.com.

What FoldCount stores, and where

  • Fold counts. For each day: how many times your iPhone was unfolded and folded, and the unfolds per hour. FoldCount does not record where you are, what you do on your iPhone, or any other content.
  • A random installation identifier, used only to keep counts from different installations apart when they sync. It is not linked to you or your Apple Account.
  • Your settings, such as whether to count unfolds only or unfolds and folds.

Without FoldCount Pro, counts are kept only in your iPhone’s memory while the app runs and are erased when it closes or the iPhone restarts. With FoldCount Pro, counts are saved on your iPhone.

iCloud

With FoldCount Pro, your counts and settings are synced through Apple’s iCloud to your private iCloud database, so they appear on your other devices and come back after a reinstall. Only you can access this data; we cannot see it. iCloud is provided by Apple and covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

You can stop syncing by turning off iCloud for FoldCount in the Settings app. You can delete your counts at any time with Settings › Reset All Counts in FoldCount, which also removes them from iCloud, or by managing iCloud storage in the Settings app.

Hinge and automations

While FoldCount is open on iPhone Duo, it reads whether the iPhone is open or closed to count unfolds and folds. Counting in the background uses Shortcuts automations that you create and that run on your iPhone. Neither sends anything to us.

Purchases

FoldCount Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we receive no payment details and no personal information about you. The app asks the App Store only whether FoldCount Pro is unlocked for your Apple Account or through Family Sharing.

Data we collect

None. FoldCount has no servers, sends no data to us or to anyone else, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated App Store and crash statistics if you have chosen to share them with app developers in your iPhone’s settings.

Children

FoldCount collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
